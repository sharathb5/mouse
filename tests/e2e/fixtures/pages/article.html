How Agents Read the Web

By A. Writer · 6 min read

Most of what we read online is surrounded by noise: navigation bars, sidebars, and advertisements compete with the paragraph you actually care about. A good assistant has to find the meaningful content first.

Semantic selection means recognizing paragraphs, sections, tables, and articles as units, then preserving their structure when the text is extracted. You can read more in the extraction guide.
